Our Story

One brand,
trusted by name.

Super Moon is the detergent powder brand manufactured by SafoneConsumer (Safone Consumer Products Bangladesh), founded in 2022 with one goal: make detergent powder that Bangladeshi families can trust, without stretching their monthly budget.

Every pack of Super Moon is produced in our own facility and certified by BSTI — the Bangladesh Standards and Testing Institution — so retailers and households know exactly what they're getting: consistent quality, batch after batch.
